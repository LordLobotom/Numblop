# Audio asset provenance

The project owner supplied the files in `audio/music/` and `audio/sfx/`. Some of the newer sound
effects were downloaded from Pixabay. Their original filenames are retained in the repository,
but the individual item URLs and contributor names are not embedded in the files and were not
available when this record was created.

Pixabay-sourced audio is used as part of the Numblop game under the Pixabay Content License:
<https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/>. The license permits free commercial and
non-commercial use, modification, and use without mandatory attribution, subject to its
prohibited uses. The audio is embedded in the game and is not distributed as standalone stock
content.

## Current presentation roles

- `backround_music.wav` — looping background music.
- `button.mp3` — compact buttons, language selection, and chest taps.
- `Menu_Select_00.wav` — answer and Settings preview selection.
- `turn_page.wav` — full-screen navigation transitions.
- `confirm_choice.wav` — calm correct-answer reinforcement.
- `giggle.mp3` — Numblop petting/reward reaction.
- `level_victor_famfare.mp3` — reward-screen entrance fanfare.
- `game-bonus.mp3` — chest opening.
- `coin2.wav` and `xp_gain.wav` — reward count-up.

`place_in_inventory.wav`, `sell_buy_item.wav`, and the duplicate `level_up.wav` are retained for
later cosmetic/progression work but are not assigned in the current loop.
