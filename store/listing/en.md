## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Meet Numblop — a cheerful green blob who loves multiplication!

Numblop turns times-table practice into a cozy daily habit. Kids play short
10-question sessions that adapt to what they already know, so practice always
feels just right — never too easy, never frustrating.

HOW IT WORKS
• Short adaptive sessions: 10 questions, mixing new facts with smart review.
• 8 times tables that unlock one by one as mastery grows.
• Three question styles that grow with the child: picking from four answers,
  then six, then typing the answer on a friendly keypad.
• Mistakes are learning moments: the full correct equation stays on screen
  until the child is ready to continue.
• No visible countdown — kids think, they don't panic.

MAKE NUMBLOP YOUR OWN
• Earn coins for every completed session.
• Spend them on body colors, hats, glasses and necklaces.
• Keep a practice streak alive and collect trophy milestones.
• A progress map shows exactly which facts are mastered.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• 100% offline — play anywhere, no internet needed or requested.
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts.
• No data collection of any kind.
• Fully available in English and Czech.

Numblop was built around one idea: five friendly minutes a day beat an hour
of drills. Give your child a practice buddy they will actually want to visit.
